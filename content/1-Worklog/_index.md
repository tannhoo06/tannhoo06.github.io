This worklog covers my 12-week internship at First Cloud AI Journey. In weeks 1–8 I learned AWS fundamentals through the [Cloud Journey](https://cloudjourney.awsfcaj.com/) labs: accounts and IAM, VPC, EC2, S3, RDS, Lightsail, containers, CloudFront, WAF, CloudWatch, Lambda, Cognito, KMS and backups. In weeks 9–12 I applied them to deploy and operate Ownword, our team's English vocabulary and writing app.
