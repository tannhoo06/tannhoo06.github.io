1. [Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global](4.1-event1/) · 26 September 2026. Teamwork under time pressure, building an AI agent on AWS and planning my learning path.
2. [AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi 2026](4.2-event2/) · 29 September 2026. Agentic AI for software development, guardrails for automation and identities that aren't people.
3. [Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner Vogels](4.3-event3/) · 2 October 2026. Matching the tool to the workload, designing for failure and reviewing AI-generated code.
